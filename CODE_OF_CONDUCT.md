# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participants must communicate respectfully and constructively. Disagreement about code and design is welcome; harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not.

Examples of positive participation include giving actionable feedback, assuming good faith, acknowledging mistakes, respecting boundaries, and keeping technical discussions focused on the work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other participation that violates this standard. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the [GrafXP GitHub profile](https://github.com/GrafXP). Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical. Maintainers are responsible for fair and proportionate enforcement.
